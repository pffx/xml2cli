Board list

The different management types are the followings:

NCY: Deployments with a single board (like a pizza box board). They do not have an architecture with shelf/LT/ IHUB NE, they are considered single NE. All Copper boards are in this category.
NCY-LT and NCY-SHELF: Deployments with 3 nodes: IHUB, and LT, and NT. Applicable to FX, MF and SF Fiber deployments. Fiber only.
NCY-ACCESS: Deployments with 2 nodes: shelf NE and LT-NE are combined into ACCESS-NE and deployment includes also iHUB. Example DFMB-C (DF-32GM). Fiber only.
